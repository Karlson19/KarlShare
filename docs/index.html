What makes it worth switching

Old sharing apps stopped trying. We did not.

01

Speed you can feel

Every file is cut into pieces and pushed across a 5GHz WiFi Direct link several pieces at a time. Two Android phones usually settle between 40 and 80 MB a second. A two hour film lands before you have found somewhere to sit.

02

Your bundle stays full

The internet never enters the picture. The two phones talk to each other and no one else, so there is no upload, no server, and nothing taken off your data plan.

03

Locked, end to end

Every link runs on TLS 1.3, and each phone confirms the other really is who it claims to be the first time they meet. No one can slip into the middle and read what you send.

04

It crosses the divide

Android to Android works today. The iPhone side, including the awkward iPhone to Android handoff that other apps fumble, is in the workshop next.

05

Nothing in the way

No banner ads. No timers. No video you sit through before you are allowed to send one photo. You open it, you send, you close it.

06

Drawn from Kente

The look borrows from Ghanaian cloth instead of the same stock icons every other app reaches for. It feels like it was made here, because it was.

Getting started

01 / INSTALL

Put it on your phone

Grab the file below and install. Your phone asks you to allow it because it did not arrive through the Play Store. That is expected.

02 / OPEN BOTH

Open it on both phones

The sender and the receiver both open Karlshare. You show up on each other's radar straight away. No codes to type, though a QR is there if you prefer it.

03 / SEND

Pick it and send

Choose your photos, videos, songs or apps, tap the person, and watch it go. Each file is checked on arrival so you know it came through clean.

Questions people ask

Is it actually free?
Yes. No paid tier, no trial that runs out, no features held back. The whole app is free.
Will it eat my data?
No. Transfers go straight between the two phones over their own WiFi link, so your data bundle is never touched.
Is my stuff safe?
Files move over an encrypted link and never pass through a server. We do not see what you send, and nobody else on the network can either.
Why does my phone warn me?
Because we hand out the file directly for now instead of through the Play Store. Allow the install when asked. A store listing is on the way.
Does it work with iPhone?
Android to Android works today. iPhone support is being built next.
Who made this?
Karlson, a student in Kumasi who got tired of ad-stuffed sharing apps and built the one he wanted to use.
